## 1. Campaign Summary

The 3PL Transformation Report is a 90-day ABM and demand generation campaign that uses an original industry research report as the primary conversion asset. The report — titled *The 3PL Digital Maturity Benchmark: What the Best 3PL Operators Are Doing Differently in 2026* — is gated, distributed through paid LinkedIn, direct SDR outreach, and email, and is designed to convert cold and warm accounts into qualified sales conversations.

The campaign targets 200 named accounts across all four regions (50 Tier 1, 150 Tier 2), with all three buyer personas engaged simultaneously through separate messaging tracks. The gated report serves three functions: it captures qualified contact data, it positions Ramco as the authoritative voice in 3PL technology strategy, and it gives SDRs a non-salesy conversation opener backed by genuine insight.

**Why this campaign, why now:**
- 3PLs in all four target regions are actively evaluating or planning technology investments in the 2026 fiscal year — the report catches them at the point of highest intent
- The "Transformation Report" framing lets Ramco lead with insight before leading with product — reducing the sales wall that generic outbound sequences hit
- A benchmarking report creates reciprocal value (the prospect gets something useful) and establishes the category frame (digital maturity as a competitive advantage) that makes Ramco's product the logical next step

## 2. Campaign Strategy

### 2.1 The Core Idea

The 3PL Digital Maturity Benchmark is a data-led research report that benchmarks how 3PL operators across the Asia Pacific and Middle East are investing in technology, what outcomes the leading operators are achieving, and where the capability gaps are costing operators in lost contracts, billing leakage, and operational inefficiency.

The report does four things for the campaign:
1. **Creates a reason to reach out** — SDRs can open with "I thought this was relevant to you" instead of "Can I tell you about Ramco?" The conversion rate on research-led outbound is 2–3× higher than product-led outbound
2. **Qualifies intent** — Downloading a benchmarking report on 3PL digital maturity is a strong intent signal; it filters for accounts who are thinking about technology investment
3. **Creates the category frame** — The report defines digital maturity as a competitive advantage, and Ramco as the platform that enables it — before the sales conversation begins
4. **Gives sales a conversation starter** — AEs can reference specific findings ("The report showed that 73% of 3PLs in your region cited billing accuracy as their top operational pain — where does that rank for you?") in their first meeting

### 2.2 Report Content Summary

The report is produced by Ramco but written as independent research. Ramco funds the study, commissions the data, and publishes under the Ramco brand with transparent methodology. It does not read as a product brochure.

**Report title:** *The 3PL Digital Maturity Benchmark 2026: What High-Performance 3PL Operators Are Doing Differently Across Asia Pacific and the Middle East*

**Report structure (12–16 pages):**
1. Executive summary — 5 headline findings
2. Study methodology — 200+ 3PL operators surveyed across AU, ID, SA, PH
3. Digital maturity model — 4-stage framework (Reactive / Structured / Connected / Predictive)
4. Finding 1: Revenue leakage — 78% of 3PLs lose 2–5% of freight under management to billing errors; leaders recover this through automated rating
5. Finding 2: Visibility gap — 69% of 3PLs cannot provide real-time shipment status without manual intervention; customers are defecting to more capable competitors
6. Finding 3: Onboarding speed — Leaders onboard new customers in under 4 weeks; laggards take 3–4 months; the gap directly affects revenue growth rate
7. Finding 4: Enterprise contract readiness — 3PLs with digital customer portals, automated invoicing, and real-time POD are winning 40% more enterprise RFPs than those without
8. Finding 5: Headcount scaling — Leaders scale revenue 25–30% with under 5% headcount growth; laggards scale revenue and headcount at the same rate
9. Regional breakdown — AU, ID, SA, PH findings compared
10. What high-maturity 3PLs do differently — 5 operational practices
11. Self-assessment tool — 10-question maturity diagnostic the reader can fill in
12. About Ramco — single back page

**Key statistics to validate before publication:**
- Billing leakage % — use Ramco customer data (anonymised) + publicly available 3PL research
- Onboarding timeline benchmark — use Ramco implementation data
- Enterprise RFP win rate correlation — use deal data from Ramco sales CRM
- Headcount scaling ratio — publicly available data from listed 3PL companies (Toll, Mainfreight, 2GO annual reports)

**Report format:** PDF (gated) + 5-page executive summary PDF (ungated, used for promotion) + LinkedIn carousel (10 slides, key findings)

### 3.3 Tier 1 Engagement Model (per account)

Each Tier 1 account receives:

**Week 1–2: Warm-up**
- Marketing: LinkedIn Matched Audience campaign (Sponsored Content) targeting all contacts at the account
- LinkedIn Insight Tag confirms account website visits — flag in HubSpot

**Week 2–3: Direct outreach (SDR)**
- SDR sends personalised LinkedIn connection request to all 3 personas at the account using the regional persona sequences from `/prompts/outbound/`
- Report referenced in Touch 1 as a reason to connect — not as a download link yet

**Week 3: Report delivery**
- SDR sends the report directly (PDF attachment + landing page link) to all engaged contacts — no form required for T1 accounts
- Personalised note: "I thought finding #4 on enterprise RFP readiness was particularly relevant to what you're doing — curious whether it matches your experience."

**Week 4–6: Nurture + AE introduction**
- Any positive response from T1 contact → AE immediately introduced
- AE sends follow-up email referencing a specific report finding relevant to the account's operation type
- AE + SDR decide: book intro call vs. send additional relevant asset first

**Week 6–12: Qualification and opportunity creation**
- AE runs discovery call using discovery question bank from `/skills/revenue/skill-06-sales-enablement-kit.md`
- Qualified → HubSpot opportunity created, deal stage = Discovery

### 3.4 Tier 2 Engagement Model

Tier 2 accounts run on a programmatic track — no AE involvement until SQL stage.

- **Week 1:** LinkedIn Matched Audience (Sponsored Content) targeting job titles matching all 3 personas at Tier 2 accounts
- **Week 2:** SDR sends LinkedIn connection request using standard regional sequence (same sequences as T1 but without account-specific personalisation)
- **Week 3:** Automated HubSpot email sequence triggered on connection accept — report download link included in email 2 of the sequence
- **Week 5+:** Any report download → immediate SDR follow-up call/message within 24 hours
- **SQL threshold:** Report download + positive LinkedIn or email response + company matches ICP → SDR qualifies → AE introduced

### 4.2 Channel Rationale

**LinkedIn Paid — primary distribution channel**
The target personas (CIO, COO, CEO) at 3PL companies are active on LinkedIn professionally. LinkedIn's job title, company, and seniority targeting is the only channel that can reliably reach a VP Operations at a named Indonesian 3PL without also wasting spend on irrelevant audiences. LinkedIn Matched Audience upload of the T1 and T2 account lists ensures the report reaches the exact accounts the sales team is working.

Budget allocation: $35K–$50K across all 4 regions for 90 days. Prioritise AU (35%) and SA (30%) given higher ACV potential per deal.

*LinkedIn campaign setup follows `/skills/acquisition/skill-07-paid-linkedin-campaigns.md` — ABM Layer 1 for T1, Persona Layer for T2.*

**SDR LinkedIn Outbound — primary T1 conversion mechanism**
For Tier 1 accounts, paid LinkedIn creates awareness and context. The direct LinkedIn outbound sequence from the SDR (or AE for SA and AU T1) converts that awareness into a conversation. The report is the SDR's opener — not a product pitch. Use sequences from `/prompts/outbound/` for each persona and region.

*SDR activity target: 15 personalised T1 outreach sequences initiated per week per region. 4 SDRs × 15 accounts/week = 60 T1 touchpoints per week across the campaign.*

**HubSpot Email — warm list reactivation**
Event attendee lists from past Ramco events (SCLAA, Saudi Logistics Expo, SCMAP) and webinar registrants who have not progressed to SQL are re-engaged with the report as a re-engagement offer. This is a low-cost, high-quality touchpoint — these contacts have already expressed some interest in Ramco.

*Email sequence: 3 touches over 21 days. Touch 1 — report announcement. Touch 2 — one key finding excerpt. Touch 3 — "Did the findings match your experience?" with meeting link.*

**Regional Events — pipeline acceleration**
Three events fall within the 90-day campaign window. The report is used as the pre-event and at-event asset — distributed to all pre-event registrant lists and used by booth staff as a conversation starter. Post-event follow-up references the report and the conversation.

### 9.3 Escalation Rules

- **T1 account responds to any outreach** → SDR notifies AE within 2 hours. AE decides next step within 4 hours.
- **T1 account downloads report** → SDR notifies AE within 24 hours. If T1 CEO persona downloads → AE or Sales Lead contacts personally within same business day.
- **MQL volume is below 50% of weekly target at Week 4** → Emergency budget reallocation meeting within 48 hours. Options: increase LinkedIn budget, expand T2 list, activate additional event sponsorships.
- **Any competitor (SAP, CargoWise, Oracle) mentioned by a prospect** → Log immediately in HubSpot `Competitor Mentioned` field. Pull relevant battlecard from `/prompts/sales-enablement/competitive-battlecard-generator.md`. Brief AE before next contact.
