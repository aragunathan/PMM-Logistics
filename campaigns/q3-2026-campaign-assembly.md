### The Q3 Strategic Narrative

Q2 launched the new TMS platform and planted Ramco's flag in the market. Q3 converts that awareness into conversations. The narrative for Q3:

> **"You've heard the pitch. Here's the proof."**

- Personalised pre-event email sent from AE (not marketing automation) 48 hours before

- All new contacts scanned/added to HubSpot on same day (event static list)

After the event (within 48 hours):
- Personalised follow-up email from AE to every contact met
- Enrol all new event contacts in HubSpot persona-matched nurture sequence
- Add all new contacts to LinkedIn retargeting audience (Skill 11, Stage 1 — post-event warm)
- Connect on LinkedIn with every new contact within 72 hours (SDR or AE — logged in HubSpot)
