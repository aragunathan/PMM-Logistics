### Likely Pain Points

1. **Multimodal billing complexity** (Operations / MD)
   Rating sea, air, and road freight under different tariff structures for the same customer is rarely automated cleanly at this scale. Manual reconciliation and billing disputes are a near-certain pain for a freight forwarder with this service mix.

2. **Fragmented visibility across modes and locations** (Operations / IT)
   Perth, Melbourne, and Adelaide operations, combined with international freight movements, require a consolidated view that most point-solution freight systems cannot provide. "Where is the shipment?" requires checking multiple systems.

3. **Customer portal and digital POD expectations** (CEO / Operations)
   Enterprise shippers (retail, FMCG, resources) increasingly expect digital proof of delivery, live shipment status, and automated invoicing. A 36-person freight forwarder without a customer-facing digital portal risks losing to larger or more technologically capable competitors.

4. **Integration debt across freight, customs, and WMS** (IT)
   Freight management, customs clearance, and warehouse management in separate systems creates ongoing integration overhead and data reconciliation cost. Every system upgrade or new customer requires an IT project.

5. **Scale constraint on the Australian operation** (CEO / MD)
   At 36 employees across three states, operational headcount growth is a real cost constraint. The ability to handle more freight volume without proportional headcount is a strategic priority.
