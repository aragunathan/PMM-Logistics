## Campaign Architecture

All campaigns run across three layers:

| Layer | Audience | Format | KPI |
|-------|----------|--------|-----|
| L1 — ABM | HubSpot matched contacts from T1/T2 account list | Sponsored Content + Message Ad | Meetings booked, pipeline influenced |
| L2 — Persona | Job title + company size + industry targeting | Sponsored Content + LGF | Demo requests, content downloads, MQLs |
| L3 — Retargeting | Website visitors, LGF openers, 50% video viewers | Sponsored Content | Conversion rate, CPL |

AU = Australia | ID = Indonesia | SA = Saudi Arabia

PERSONA: COO / CEO / CIO / All
CAMPAIGN: C01 / C02 / C03 / C04 / C05

## Audience Specs by Region

### Australia
- Operations Persona: COO / VP Ops / Head of Transport / Head of Warehousing
- CEO Persona: CEO / MD / President / Owner
- CIO Persona: CIO / VP IT / Director IT
- Industry: Transportation, Trucking & Railroad / Logistics & Supply Chain / Warehousing
- Company size: 201–5,000 employees
- Target audience size per persona: 5,000–25,000

### Indonesia
- Same title sets as above
- Add: General Manager Operations / Direktur Operasional (for regional title variants)
- Company size: 201–5,000 employees
- Target audience size per persona: 8,000–40,000
- Language: English targeting only at LinkedIn ad level; sales follow-up bilingual

### Saudi Arabia
- Same title sets as above
- Add: VP Logistics / Director Supply Chain (common in KSA corporate structures)
- Company size: 201–5,000 employees
- Target audience size per persona: 3,000–15,000

**Audience settings (all campaigns, all regions):**
- Audience Expansion: OFF
- LinkedIn Audience Network: OFF for ABM; test ON for Persona layer only
- Exclusions (always): current Ramco customers, HubSpot contacts with meeting booked status, unsubscribers

## HubSpot Integration

- All LGF submissions sync to HubSpot via LinkedIn Ads integration
- Lead source = `LinkedIn Paid | [Campaign Name] | [Region]`
- Auto-enroll in HubSpot workflow: `LinkedIn Lead → SDR Assignment → 48hr follow-up task`
- MQL threshold: content download + company size ≥201 employees + title match to persona
- Demo request = immediate SDR assignment, SLA: contact within 4 business hours
