## Campaign Brief

**Objective:** Generate qualified demo requests and content downloads from 3PL operations leaders experiencing revenue leakage from manual billing, rate disputes, and fragmented invoicing processes.

**Core pain targeted:** Operations leaders at mid-large 3PLs ($100M+ revenue) lose 2–5% of top-line revenue annually to billing errors, invoice disputes, and manual rate reconciliation. They know the pain exists but often cannot quantify it — and that's the entry point.

**Offer / CTA:** 3PL Revenue Leakage Calculator (LGF download) → SDR follow-up within 48 hours

**PPCO logic:**
- **Pain:** Revenue is leaving through billing disputes, credits, and manual errors every month
- **Proof:** 3PL reference — billing disputes reduced, revenue recovered (use regional reference where available)
- **Change:** Automated rating and billing eliminates the leak and gives ops leaders a number they can defend to the CEO
- **Offer:** 5-minute calculator that shows them the annual cost of their current billing process

**Why this generates qualified pipeline:** Any COO who downloads this and completes the calculator has self-identified as someone with a billing problem. The SDR follow-up is: "What did the number come out to for your business?" — not a cold pitch.
