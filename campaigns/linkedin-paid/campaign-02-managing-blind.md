## Campaign Brief

**Objective:** Generate demo requests and content downloads from 3PL operations leaders who are running their operations without a unified real-time view — relying on calls, WhatsApp messages, spreadsheets, and gut instinct instead of live data.

**Core pain targeted:** COOs and operations directors at $100M+ 3PLs cannot see shipment status, warehouse capacity, and fleet location in a single view. Customer calls asking "where is my shipment?" require manual digging. SLA misses happen because the system can't warn them in time. This is the most universally felt pain across all three regions — but the specific flavour varies.

**Offer / CTA:**
- Awareness: 5-minute product walkthrough video (no form)
- Mid-funnel: "What's Your Biggest Visibility Gap?" Conversation Ad → persona-specific content
- Demo: 20-minute operations-specific demo request (LGF)

**PPCO logic:**
- **Pain:** "Where is my shipment?" — your team answers this manually, every day
- **Proof:** Operations leaders at 3PLs using Ramco eliminated this call entirely (specific regional reference)
- **Change:** One live operational view across transport, warehouse, and fleet — for your team and your customers
- **Offer:** 5-min product walkthrough → then demo booking

**Why this generates qualified pipeline:** Visibility pain is urgent, emotional, and directly tied to customer relationship risk. Any COO who engages with this content has a real problem. The Conversation Ad branches by pain type — allowing the SDR to lead with the right angle.

## Video Ad Script — All Regions (30 seconds, subtitled)

**Visual direction:** Fast-cut sequence. No voiceover in first 3 seconds — let the visuals and subtitle text do the work (80% of LinkedIn video watched without sound).

**Frame 1 (0–3 sec):** Phone ringing on a desk. Text overlay: *"Where's my shipment?"*
**Frame 2 (3–8 sec):** Operations person opening three different software screens. Text: *"One call. Three systems. Still no answer."*
**Frame 3 (8–15 sec):** Cut to a clean single dashboard — map view with live fleet positions, warehouse occupancy bar, shipment status feed. Text: *"Your whole operation. One view. Live."*
**Frame 4 (15–25 sec):** Dashboard zooms in — a late shipment flagged automatically, proactive customer notification already sent. Text: *"Ramco alerts your team before your customer calls."*
**Frame 5 (25–30 sec):** End card — Ramco logo + tagline: *"See your whole 3PL. Not three systems."* CTA text: *"Watch the 5-minute demo →"*

**Intro text (above video):**
> For 3PL operations leaders: the cost of not seeing your operation in real time isn't just internal chaos — it's every customer call you shouldn't have to take.

**Headline:** See Your Whole 3PL — Fleet, Warehouse, Shipments. One View.

**CTA Button:** Watch Demo → 5-minute product walkthrough (hosted on Ramco site or LinkedIn)
