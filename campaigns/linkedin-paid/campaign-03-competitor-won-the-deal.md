## Campaign Brief

**Objective:** Surface and convert CEO-level awareness of the competitive risk of digital capability gaps — specifically the risk of losing or failing to win enterprise shipping contracts because the 3PL cannot demonstrate real-time visibility, digital POD, automated invoicing, and customer portals.

**Core pain targeted:** 3PL CEOs are losing RFPs and contract renewals not on price and not on relationships — but on digital capability. Enterprise shippers (retail, FMCG, government, manufacturing) are now requiring technology capability as table stakes. The CEO who hasn't yet felt this knows it's coming. The CEO who has already lost a deal to it is ready to act.

**Offer / CTA:**
- Awareness: "What Australian/Indonesian/Saudi 3PLs Are Winning With" — case study (LGF download)
- ABM Direct: Executive briefing request (20-minute call with senior Ramco leader)
- Mid-funnel retargeting: CEO ROI guide — "The Business Case for 3PL Technology Investment"

**PPCO logic:**
- **Pain:** "We lost that RFP — not on price, on capability." Enterprise shippers want digital and you can't show it.
- **Proof:** Named 3PL reference — won enterprise contract after implementing Ramco (regional, same revenue tier)
- **Change:** A digitally capable 3PL wins deals it couldn't win before — and retains accounts it would otherwise lose
- **Offer:** Case study of a peer CEO in the same region — what they did and what they won

**Why this generates qualified pipeline:** CEO pain here is strategic, not operational. It creates urgency around an event (a lost deal, a pending RFP, a board conversation about growth) that the SDR can surface in follow-up. The ask is a conversation with a senior Ramco leader — peer to peer.
