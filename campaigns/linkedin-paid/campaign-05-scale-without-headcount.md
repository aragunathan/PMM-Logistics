## Campaign Brief

**Objective:** Convert both CEO and COO personas simultaneously by targeting the shared pain of a business model where revenue growth requires proportional headcount growth — eroding margin and making the business operationally fragile.

**Core pain targeted:**
- **CEO angle:** Every new customer win requires more people. EBITDA margin doesn't improve as the business grows — it stays flat or contracts. The business is scaling complexity, not efficiency.
- **COO angle:** The team is smart and capable but exhausted from manually managing what software should handle. Automation isn't about cutting people — it's about letting good people do work that matters.

**Offer / CTA:**
- CEO: "The 3PL Scalability Guide — How to Grow Revenue Without Growing Cost-to-Serve" (LGF download)
- COO: "5-Minute 3PL Operations Demo — See What Automation Changes Day-to-Day"
- ABM: Executive briefing request

**PPCO logic (CEO variant):**
- **Pain:** Every growth phase requires a proportional headcount increase. Margin stays flat.
- **Proof:** 3PL reference at similar revenue size — grew 30% revenue with same operations headcount after implementing Ramco
- **Change:** Scalable operations platform decouples revenue growth from headcount growth
- **Offer:** Scalability guide + peer CEO conversation

**PPCO logic (COO variant):**
- **Pain:** The team is smart and experienced — but they spend half their time doing what software should automate
- **Proof:** Operational case study — specific metrics: dispatch time, exception handling, manual process hours saved
- **Change:** Operations team does work that matters; exceptions handled by the system, not people
- **Offer:** 5-minute demo focused on day-in-the-life operations, not feature lists

**Why this generates qualified pipeline:** This campaign hits the strategic pain of every 3PL CEO who has looked at a flat EBITDA margin despite revenue growth. The COO variant gives the operations champion a concrete demo path. Together they create a joint entry point — CEO downloads the guide, COO books the demo, SDR has two entry points into the same account.

## Dual-Persona Account Tracking

This campaign intentionally targets both CEO and COO at the same accounts. HubSpot tracking:

- Tag both records with campaign: `C05 | Scale Without Headcount`
- If CEO downloads guide AND COO books demo from same company: flag as `Hot Account — Dual Engagement` in HubSpot
- Trigger: Notify AE immediately for same-day outreach — do not wait for standard SDR SLA
- Goal: Use dual engagement as the hook — "I noticed both [CEO name] and [COO name] at [Company] have been exploring this — would a joint conversation be useful?"
