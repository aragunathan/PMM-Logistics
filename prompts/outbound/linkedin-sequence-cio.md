# LinkedIn Outbound Sequences — CIO / VP IT Persona
## Ramco Logistics Software | Outbound Prompts

> **Persona:** CIO, VP IT, Director IT at 3PL companies with $100M+ annual revenue
> **Objective:** Book a 30-minute technical discovery call or platform architecture walkthrough
> **Rules applied:**
> - No pitch in Touch 1 — connection note establishes relevance only
> - No mention of Ramco in Touch 1 or Touch 2 unless the contact has already engaged
> - One CTA per message, maximum
> - Value before ask — every message gives something before it requests something
> - All messages are immediately sendable — no brackets, no placeholders
> - Regional logistics context woven into every sequence
> - Read `/inputs/persona-definitions.md` CIO section before using these sequences
> - All sequences align with messaging pillars in `/skills/activation/skill-03-persona-messaging-architecture.md`

## Usage Notes

**Before sending any sequence:**
1. Verify the contact's current title and company on LinkedIn — confirm they still hold the role
2. Check HubSpot — ensure the contact is not already in an active deal or enrolled in another sequence
3. Personalise Touch 1 to reflect something specific to their company or LinkedIn activity if possible — the templates above are starting points, not scripts
4. Confirm the sender's LinkedIn profile is complete: professional photo, accurate title, recent activity — a sparse profile kills connection acceptance rates

**Sequence management in HubSpot:**
- Build as a Sales Sequence (not a Workflow) so the sequence pauses automatically when a contact replies
- Assign the sequence to the regional rep who will own the relationship — do not send from a generic account
- Log every LinkedIn interaction as an activity on the HubSpot contact record

**When a contact replies at any touch:**
- Pause the sequence immediately
- Respond within 4 business hours
- Route to the next logical conversation: if they express interest in a demo, book directly; if they ask a technical question, answer it thoroughly and offer a live conversation to go deeper
- Update `abm_sequence_status` in HubSpot to "Replied"

**When a contact accepts the connection but never responds to messages:**
- After Touch 5, move to LinkedIn ad retargeting (ensure they are in the matched audience — see `skill-07-paid-linkedin-campaigns.md`)
- Add to the persona-matched email nurture sequence if their email is available (see `skill-04-email-nurture-sequences.md`)
- Revisit with a fresh outreach angle in 90 days — a new regulatory development, a new case study, or a relevant event invitation

**Regional timing reminders:**
- Australia: Avoid contact on Melbourne Cup day (first Tuesday November), ANZAC Day, and the Christmas shutdown period (mid-December to mid-January)
- Indonesia: Reduce cadence during Ramadan; avoid Lebaran week entirely; pause all outreach on Indonesian National Day (August 17)
- Saudi Arabia: Sunday–Thursday work week only; avoid Friday and Saturday; reduce activity during Ramadan and in the week around Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha
- Philippines: Avoid Holy Week (Semana Santa — 4–5 days in March/April); reduce activity from December 16 onward as the Christmas season begins in earnest
