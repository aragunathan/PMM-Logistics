# Prompt 4 — Generate Landing Page Copy

**When to use:** Run this before launching a new campaign or when Quality Score on a campaign drops below 7.

---

## Complete Prompt

Write conversion-optimised landing page copy for Ramco Logistics Software.

Module: [REPLACE: TMS / WMS]
Region: [REPLACE: Australia / Indonesia / Saudi Arabia / Philippines / India]
Persona arriving from ad: [REPLACE: CIO / COO / CEO]
Ad headline that sent them here: [REPLACE WITH EXACT AD HEADLINE]

Read inputs/persona-definitions.md before writing.

Deliver exactly this:

H1: Must mirror the ad headline intent. Max 10 words.
    Must contain the module name and either 3PL or the region.
Subheadline: Max 20 words. Lead with a measurable outcome.
3 bullet points: Each must start with a verb. Outcome-focused.
    No feature lists. Max 12 words per bullet.
Social proof element: One customer quote or one stat.
    You may write a plausible placeholder — mark it [PLACEHOLDER].
Form headline: Max 8 words.
Primary CTA button text: Max 4 words.
Secondary CTA for not-ready visitors: Max 8 words.

Rules:
- The H1 must match the ad headline or Quality Score will drop
- Write for the named persona — not generic SaaS copy
- Do not use: world-class, cutting-edge, powerful, robust, seamless
- Every claim must be specific and believable
