# LinkedIn Ad Copy — Complete Library and Generator
## Ramco Logistics Software | Paid Ads

> **Purpose:** Ready-to-use LinkedIn ad copy for all three personas × all four regions × multiple ad formats. Use the copy sets directly or use the generator prompts at the end of this file to produce new variants.
> **Formats covered:** Sponsored Content (Single Image), Lead Gen Form (LGF), Message Ad (InMail), Conversation Ad opening, Video Ad script
> **Rules applied to all copy:**
> - Never open intro text with "Ramco", "We", or "I"
> - No superlatives: no world-class, industry-leading, best-in-class, cutting-edge, seamless, robust, revolutionary
> - Every ad references a specific 3PL pain or outcome — no generic logistics statements
> - One CTA per ad, maximum
> - Intro text under 150 characters (visible before "see more")
> - Headline under 70 characters (shows without truncation)
> - Message Ad body 300–500 characters
> - All copy is immediately usable — no brackets, no placeholders
> - Read `/inputs/persona-definitions.md` and the approved messaging matrix in `skill-03` before creating new variants

---

## HOW TO USE THIS FILE

**For immediate deployment:** Go to the relevant section (persona → region → format) and copy the ad directly into LinkedIn Campaign Manager.

**For A/B testing:** Each section contains 3 variants labelled by angle (Pain-led / Outcome-led / Proof-led / Curiosity-led). Run two variants per campaign and identify the winning angle before scaling budget.

**For new copy:** Use the Claude prompts in the final section of this file.

**Format guide:**
- `[SC]` = Sponsored Content (Single Image Ad)
- `[LGF]` = Lead Gen Form
- `[MA]` = Message Ad (InMail)
- `[CA]` = Conversation Ad opening message
- `[VA]` = Video Ad script (30 seconds)
