# Prompt 1 — Generate Full Annual Marketing Plan

Use once per year in Q4 to generate a complete, board-ready annual marketing plan for the following year.

---PROMPT START---
You are the head of marketing for Ramco Systems Logistics Software.
Read these files before starting:
- inputs/input.json
- inputs/persona-definitions.md
- strategy/strategy.md
- analytics/kpi-dashboard.md
- strategy/competitive/master-competitor-list.md
- skills/ops/skill-13-annual-marketing-plan.md

Here is my current year performance data:
[PASTE: channel performance, pipeline generated, top wins, top losses]

Here is my budget for next year:
[PASTE: total budget in USD]

Here is my headcount plan for next year:
[PASTE: team size and roles]

Here are my top 3 priorities from sales leadership for next year:
[PASTE: e.g. "break into Saudi Arabia", "double Philippines pipeline", "improve demo-to-opp rate"]

Using all frameworks in skill-13-annual-marketing-plan.md, generate a
complete annual marketing plan for [YEAR] covering all 10 sections:
1. Situation analysis
2. Pipeline and revenue targets
3. Organic and SEO plan
4. Paid media plan
5. Events and conferences plan
6. Partnerships plan
7. Social media plan
8. Competitive monitoring plan
9. Innovative programs (select top 3 for this year based on budget)
10. Brand visibility plan
11. Budget allocation and headcount

Make every section specific to Ramco, our 3 personas, and our 5 regions (Australia, Indonesia, Saudi Arabia, Philippines, India).
No generic marketing advice. Every recommendation must reference a specific
skill file, prompt file, or agent file from this system.

Save the complete plan to: strategy/annual-plan-[YEAR].md

At the end, produce a 1-page executive summary (CEO/board ready)
covering: pipeline target, budget, top 5 initiatives, quarterly milestones.
---PROMPT END---
