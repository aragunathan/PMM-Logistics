## The 3 Non-Negotiable Rules for Enterprise SaaS Ad Copy

Rule 1 — Mirror the keyword intent exactly
If someone searches "TMS software for 3PL Australia" your headline
must contain those exact words or a very close variant.
Google rewards this with lower CPCs and higher ad rank.

Rule 2 — Lead with outcomes not features
"Reduce transport costs by 20%" beats "Advanced TMS with 200 features"
for every COO and CEO reading the ad.
Features only matter to CIOs — keep one feature-led variant per ad group.

Rule 3 — Make the CTA sound effortless
"Book a Demo" beats "Contact Us"
"See It in 30 Minutes" beats "Learn More"
The lower the friction sounds, the higher the click-to-conversion rate.

## Google RSA Format Rules
Each Responsive Search Ad contains:
- Headlines: up to 15 (max 30 characters each)
- Descriptions: up to 4 (max 90 characters each)
- Display URL paths: 2 fields (max 15 characters each)
Google automatically tests combinations. Write enough strong variants
that every combination Google tests is still relevant and compelling.

## Character Count Rule
Every headline must be 30 characters or fewer.
Every description must be 90 characters or fewer.
Flag any line that exceeds these limits before saving.

## DESCRIPTION FRAMEWORK

Write exactly 4 descriptions per ad group — one per angle below.
Character limit: 90 max per description. Count carefully.

### Angle 1 — Pain and Solution
Formula: [Specific pain] + [Ramco solves it] + [CTA]
Purpose: Resonates with buyers who are frustrated right now.

TMS Pain and Solution description:
"Managing transport & freight on separate tools? Ramco unifies everything for 3PLs. Book a demo."
(79 chars ✓)

WMS Pain and Solution description:
"Multi-client warehouse on legacy WMS? Ramco gives 3PLs real-time inventory control. Demo today."
(88 chars ✓)

### Angle 2 — Outcome and Proof
Formula: [Measurable outcome] + [Who achieved it] + [CTA]
Purpose: Builds credibility with buyers who need evidence.

TMS Outcome and Proof description:
"3PLs using Ramco cut cost per shipment & eliminate billing disputes. Get your 30-min demo."
(88 chars ✓)

WMS Outcome and Proof description:
"3PLs using Ramco WMS report higher throughput & lower labour costs. Book a personalised demo."
(90 chars ✓)

### Angle 3 — Differentiator and CTA
Formula: [What makes Ramco unique] + [CTA]
Purpose: Works well for buyers who have already seen competitor ads.

TMS Differentiator description:
"Only integrated TMS + WMS + Fleet built for 3PLs. Trusted across APAC & Middle East. Demo today."
(90 chars ✓)

WMS Differentiator description:
"WMS for 3PL complexity — multi-client, multi-site, multi-currency. Integrates with TMS. See it live."
(90 chars ✓)

### Angle 4 — Urgency and Specificity
Formula: [Cost of inaction] + [Specific next step]
Purpose: Motivates buyers who have been researching but not acting.

TMS Urgency description:
"Still on fragmented logistics tools? Your margin is slipping. See Ramco's integrated TMS platform today."
(90 chars ✓)

## LANDING PAGE ALIGNMENT RULES

### Why This Matters
If your ad headline says "TMS Software for 3PLs Australia" but the
landing page H1 says "Ramco Logistics Software Solutions" — Google
lowers your Quality Score. Lower Quality Score = higher CPC by 40-60%.

### Rule: Ad Headline Must Match Landing Page H1
Every ad group landing page H1 must mirror the ad headline intent.

Current Ramco pages and required H1 updates:

TMS page (ramco.com/products/logistics-software/transportation-management-tms/)
Current H1: [check current page]
Required H1: "TMS Software Built Exclusively for 3PL Companies"

WMS page (ramco.com/products/logistics-software/warehouse-management-wms/)
Current H1: [check current page]
Required H1: "WMS Software Built Exclusively for 3PL Companies"

### Minimum Landing Page Requirements Per Campaign
Each landing page must have ALL of these before campaign goes live:

[ ] H1 matches the ad headline intent
[ ] Demo booking form visible without scrolling (above the fold)
[ ] Form has max 4 fields: Name, Company, Work Email, Phone
[ ] At least 1 customer logo or quote visible above the fold
[ ] Single clear CTA — not 5 different buttons competing
[ ] Page loads in under 3 seconds (test at pagespeed.web.dev)
[ ] Mobile layout tested — majority of APAC clicks are mobile

### Recommended Landing Page Copy Structure

SECTION 1 — Above the fold:
H1: [Module] Software for 3PLs in [Region]
Subhead: [Outcome — e.g., "Cut transport costs and gain real-time visibility across your network"]
Form: Name / Company / Email / Phone / "Book My 30-Minute Demo"
Trust element: 2-3 customer logos OR 1 short customer quote

SECTION 2 — Below the fold:
3 outcome-focused bullet points (not feature lists)
Example bullets:
- Reduce cost per shipment with automated route optimisation
- Eliminate billing disputes with real-time rate calculation
- Get full fleet and freight visibility from one dashboard

SECTION 3:
Short 60-second product walkthrough video (optional but +30% conversion lift)

SECTION 4:

## FAILURE MODES

Failure 1: Ad headline does not match landing page H1
Result: Quality Score drops. CPC increases by 40-60% within 2 weeks.
Fix: Update landing page H1 to mirror the ad headline intent before launch.
How to check: Google Ads → Keywords → Quality Score column →
click on score → see "Landing page experience" rating.

Failure 2: All 12 headlines are in the same category
Result: Google runs out of meaningful combinations to test.
Low-performing ad strength score. Poor CTR.
Fix: Always write across all 4 categories:
3 Keyword-Match + 4 Outcome + 3 Differentiator + 2 CTA.

Failure 3: Descriptions are longer than 90 characters
Result: Google truncates mid-sentence. Ad looks broken. CTR drops.
Fix: Count characters before saving. Use charactercounter.com to verify.
Rule: If in doubt, cut it. Shorter is better for descriptions.

Failure 4: Same ad copy running in all regions
Result: Indonesian buyers see Australian city references.
Saudi buyers see no Vision 2030 context. CTR and conversion drop.
Fix: Every region gets its own ad set. Minimum 1 regional reference
per ad set in at least 2 headlines.

Failure 5: Pinning too many headlines
Result: Pinning locks headlines into positions and prevents Google
from testing combinations. Ad strength drops to Poor.
Fix: Only pin Position 1 (keyword-match) and Position 3 (CTA).
Leave all other 10 headlines unpinned.

Failure 6: Not monitoring asset-level performance
Result: A weak headline drags down the whole ad without you knowing.
Fix: Every Monday go to Google Ads → Ads → your RSA → Asset details.
Google labels each asset: Learning / Low / Good / Best.
Replace any headline or description rated Low after 500+ impressions.
