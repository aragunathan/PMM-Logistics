# SKILL FILE 12: Competitive Intel
Category: Revenue
Save to: skills/revenue/skill-12-competitive-intel.md

## When to Use
Run this skill whenever:
- A prospect mentions a competitor during discovery or evaluation
- A competitor appears in a new region Ramco is targeting
- Pricing or capability objections come up in deals
- A new competitor is identified through market research or lost deal analysis

## Competitors to Track for Ramco Logistics

### Tier 1 Competitors (Direct — Enterprise 3PL TMS/WMS)
- Oracle Transportation Management (Oracle TM)
- SAP Transportation Management (SAP TM)
- Blue Yonder (formerly JDA) TMS + WMS
- Manhattan Associates WMS

### Tier 2 Competitors (Regional or Partial Overlap)
- MercuryGate TMS (strong in North America, growing APAC)
- Descartes Systems (strong in AU and ID for transport)
- WiseTech Global / CargoWise (strong in AU — freight forwarding focused)
- Körber WMS (warehouse-heavy, growing in APAC)

### Tier 3 Competitors (Local or Emerging)
- Local ERP vendors in Indonesia (Odoo, Sage, local builds)
- Local logistics software vendors in Saudi Arabia and Philippines
- In-house built systems (common in large family-owned 3PLs in PH and ID)
- FarEye (India-based, strong in last-mile delivery visibility and dispatch — growing mid-market presence)
- Locus (India-based, route optimisation and dispatch management — positioned as AI-driven logistics intelligence)
- Custom-built systems (common in large Indian 3PLs where internal IT teams built proprietary TMS/WMS; difficult to displace but often fails at compliance scale — GST e-way bill, FASTag — which creates entry opportunity)

## What Makes Ramco Unique vs All Competitors
1. Built exclusively for 3PLs — not a generic ERP adapted for logistics
2. Fully integrated TMS + WMS + Fleet + Hub Management + Rating and Billing in one platform
3. Strong implementation presence in APAC and Middle East — local support teams
4. Faster implementation timeline vs Oracle and SAP (enterprise tier)
5. Purpose-built for multi-client, multi-site 3PL operations

## Battlecard Format (used in Step 3 of agent workflow)
For each competitor:
- One-line summary of who they are
- Where they win (be honest — this builds credibility)
- Where Ramco wins (specific, not generic)
- Top 3 Ramco differentiators vs this competitor
- 3 discovery questions that surface this competitor's weaknesses naturally
- 5 objection handling scripts (objection + Ramco response)
- What to say when prospect says they are already using this competitor
