## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY — CEO / BOARD READY

**Pipeline target (marketing-sourced):** $14M (35% of $40M total pipeline goal)
**Total marketing budget:** $110,000 USD
**Team:** 3 people — PMM, Outbound, Regional Marketing ANZ
**Budget-to-pipeline ratio:** 0.28% — extremely lean. Every dollar tied to a named account or measurable outcome.

**The big picture:** In 2025 Ramco generated $19M in pipeline with Events and Organic as the top two channels. $2.5M closed won, $10M lost (primarily on price), ~$6.5M in active pipeline. In 2026 the goal is to generate $40M in **net new pipeline measured as Total Contract Value (TCV) — not ARR.** At a 5-year average contract, $40M TCV = ~$8M ARR if 100% closed — consistent with the strategy.md ARR target. The $6.5M active pipeline from 2025 is tracked separately in HubSpot and not counted toward the 2026 target.

**Channel strategy:** 60% of pipeline from digital (organic + paid + outbound), 40% from events. Events performed strongly in 2025 and remain critical — but 2026 builds the digital engine so Ramco is not solely dependent on event attendance for pipeline.

**Top 5 initiatives for 2026:**

1. Organic and content acceleration — double blog output, build regional landing pages, ANZ keyword rankings; organic targets 30% of total TCV pipeline ($12M); highest ROI channel
2. ANZ event and roundtable programme — VTA, QTA, Transport and Logistics Expo, SCLAA; events target 40% of TCV pipeline ($16M); proven top channel from 2025
3. Outbound performance fix — email open rate at 5% (benchmark: 25%+); fix domain authentication and rewrite sequences by end of Q1 before scaling volume
4. Google Ads ANZ launch — $22K allocated to high-intent TMS/WMS search terms; Q1 launch and learn, Q2 optimise; targets $6M TCV pipeline
5. Reference programme — VISY Logistics (ANZ, confirmed) and CKB Logistics (Indonesia, confirmed) deployed across all channels from Q1; every ANZ prospect gets a VISY reference, every Indonesia prospect gets CKB

**Quarterly milestones:**

- Q1: Fix outbound (email open rate to 15%+), launch Google Ads ANZ, publish VISY case study, build AU regional landing pages
- Q2: TMS platform launch campaign, 2 ANZ roundtable events, ROI calculator live, LinkedIn organic to 4 posts/week
- Q3: Indonesia content and outbound expansion, Saudi Arabia light-touch campaigns, Philippines assessment
- Q4: Year-end demo push, keyword review, H2 pipeline close support, 2027 plan draft

**Critical clarification:** $40M is Total Contract Value (TCV), not ARR. At a 5-year average contract and $150K ACV, each deal = $750K TCV. $40M TCV ÷ $750K per deal = ~53 qualified opportunities needed in pipeline for the year. Marketing-sourced at 35% = ~19 opportunities from marketing. This is a realistic target for a 3-person team and $110K budget. At 20% win rate on $40M TCV pipeline = $8M TCV closed = ~$1.6M net new ARR per year, which feeds toward the cumulative $8M ARR target alongside existing base ARR.

**Budget constraint flag:** $110K is the tightest budget in this system relative to the pipeline target. The plan does not include large event sponsorships, broad awareness campaigns, or agency retainers. Every line item must be justified by expected pipeline.

  Command: "Run the event prep agent for [EVENT NAME] in [REGION]"

5. Review pipeline at 30, 60, and 90 days post-event
6. Decision rule: less than 2× cost in pipeline by 90 days → downgrade to Tier 2 in 2027
