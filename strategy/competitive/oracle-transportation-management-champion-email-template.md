# Internal Champion Email Template — Oracle TM vs Ramco

For internal use: AE sends this to their CIO or COO champion to help them
justify choosing Ramco over Oracle TM to the CEO or board.
Last updated: 2026-03-24

---

## Instructions for AE

Send this template to your internal champion (CIO or COO) after a positive
evaluation meeting. Ask them to adapt and send it internally to the CEO or
board decision maker. Fill in all [PLACEHOLDERS] before sending.

Keep the tone peer-level — this should read like an executive writing to
a colleague, not a vendor's marketing copy.

---

## Email Template

**Subject:** TMS/WMS evaluation — my recommendation

Hi [CEO FIRST NAME],

After completing our evaluation, I want to share my recommendation before
we meet next week.

We looked seriously at Oracle TM given our existing Oracle ERP investment.
Here is why I am recommending Ramco instead for [COMPANY NAME]:

**1. Built for how we actually operate.**
Oracle TM was designed for shippers moving their own freight. We are a
multi-client 3PL. Ramco's billing, customer portals, and operational
workflows are built for exactly our model — without the customisation
Oracle would require.

**2. We go live faster and own the system.**
Oracle TM at our scale means an 18-month SI engagement and ongoing
consulting fees for configuration changes. Ramco goes live in 6–12 months
with their own team, and our ops team configures it directly after go-live.

**3. Lower five-year total cost.**
When you factor in Oracle WMS as a separate licence, SI implementation,
and ongoing SI support, Oracle's five-year TCO is significantly higher.
Ramco is one platform, one contract, one team.

I am confident this is the right decision for [COMPANY NAME] in [REGION].
Happy to walk you through the full evaluation summary before the board
presentation.

[CHAMPION NAME]
[CHAMPION TITLE]

---

## AE Usage Notes

- Replace all [PLACEHOLDERS] with real names before sending to champion
- Suggest the champion adapts the three points to reference specific
operational details from their own evaluation — the more specific, the more
credible it reads internally
- This email works best when sent 1–2 weeks before the final board or CEO
decision meeting
- Do not send this directly to the CEO yourself — it must come from the
internal champion to be credible
